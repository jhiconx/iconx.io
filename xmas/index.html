4. HOW TO ENTER

No purchase or payment is necessary. Eligible participants may enter through the SMS activation and redemption process:

  • Text CHILI: Send CHILI to 903-868-1073 during the Sweepstakes Period. Iconx issues one (1) introductory CHI reward once per unique mobile number and sends an activation link.
  • Activate: Open the activation link and send the pre-populated ACTIVATE REWARDS text. Following a qualifying activation, Iconx credits ten (10) CHI rewards to the participant's phone-linked wallet.
  • Gift-card drawing entry: After successful activation, five (5) CHI are automatically redeemed from the participant's wallet for entry into the applicable weekly $50 H-E-B Gift Card drawing, subject to eligibility and transaction verification.
  • Entry limit: One qualifying activation and gift-card drawing entry per eligible mobile number for the promotion. Duplicate messages or repeated activation attempts do not generate additional entries.

Standard carrier message and data rates may apply. No product purchase is required to receive the initial reward or activate the gift-card drawing entry.
